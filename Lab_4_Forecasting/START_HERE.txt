THE ONE RULE THAT SHAPES THIS LAB
  You are forecasting SEVEN DAYS AHEAD, because that is when the train and crew
  plan gets published. So when you forecast next Tuesday, you do not know
  Monday's ridership -- you know nothing from the last seven days.
  Every feature you build must be at least 7 days old.
  If a change makes your score suddenly much better, check that rule first.
  That is the classic forecasting mistake, and it is called leakage.
  It applies to more than model features: it also binds the baselines, and any
  threshold or safety margin you tune. The Challenge has a trap about exactly
  this -- see whether you spot it before the notebook tells you.
